Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 9, 2026

This policy explains what information The Paper Atelier ("we", "us") collects when you visit this website, why we collect it, and the choices you have. By using this site, you agree to the practices described below.

1. Who we are

The Paper Atelier is a digital product brand selling downloadable craft patterns and courses. Our store checkout is operated on Shopify. If you have questions about this policy or your data, contact us at support@thepaperatelier.shop.

2. Information we collect

  • Information you provide: name, email and shipping/billing details entered at checkout (collected and processed by Shopify, our checkout provider).
  • Automatic browsing data: IP address, device and browser type, pages viewed, referring URL, and approximate location, collected via cookies and similar technologies.
  • Campaign data: UTM parameters (source, medium, campaign) and click identifiers from the ads or links that brought you here, used to measure which campaigns work.

3. Cookies and tracking technologies

We use cookies, local storage and pixel tags to run this site and to measure and improve our advertising. This currently includes the Meta (Facebook) Pixel, delivered through our analytics partner UTMify, which helps us understand ad performance and show relevant ads to people who have visited this site. If we run Google Ads campaigns, Google's advertising and remarketing tags may be added here as well.

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Doing so may affect how parts of the site work.

4. How we use your information

  • To process and deliver your order through Shopify.
  • To measure which ads and pages lead to purchases, so we can improve them.
  • To show you relevant ads for this product on platforms like Meta and Google (remarketing).
  • To respond to support requests you send us.
  • To comply with legal and tax obligations related to sales.

5. How we share your information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with the service providers that help us run the store and our advertising, including:

  • Shopify — checkout, payment processing and order fulfillment.
  • UTMify and Meta (Facebook) — ad measurement and remarketing.
  • Google — ad measurement and remarketing, if Google Ads campaigns are active.

These providers process data under their own privacy policies and only for the purposes described here.

6. Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live (for example, under the GDPR in the UK/EU or the CCPA in California), you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or request a copy of the personal data we hold about you, and to object to or restrict certain processing. To exercise any of these rights, email support@thepaperatelier.shop. We will respond within a reasonable timeframe.

7. Data retention

We keep order information for as long as needed for accounting, tax and customer-support purposes. Browsing and campaign data collected via cookies is retained only for the period needed for the measurement purposes described above, or as set by the relevant provider.

8. Children's privacy

This site is not directed at children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy or your data? Email support@thepaperatelier.shop.
